# SoloOS product specification

**One Founder. One Company. One Intelligent Operating System.**

## Objective and users
A skill-first operating aid for solo founders combining business intelligence,
management reporting and explicitly authorized business execution. Serve SaaS,
e-commerce, creator, freelance, consulting, digital-products and custom businesses.
The design prioritizes evidence-aware decisions and consistent cross-department
figures. Originality is a design goal, not a claim that no related product exists.

## Implemented release scope
1. Onboarding and unified validated data with provenance and evidence classification.
2. Eight department reviews with distinct findings and cross-functional priorities.
3. Universal financial KPIs, six business KPI templates and safe custom arithmetic.
4. Daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly and annual reporting.
5. Explicit cash scenarios with bounded assumptions and evidence prerequisites.
6. Decision logs, actual-versus-target evaluation and optional SQLite persistence.
7. Proposal-first GitHub issue creation with scoped expiring approvals, separate
   high-risk confirmation, idempotency handling and audit records.
8. Standalone skill, portable plugin, private marketplace and reproducible packaging.

## Acceptance criteria
- Preserve legacy Phase 1 results and accept existing 0.1 data.
- Validate entity references, duplicate IDs/measurements, timezone and monetary precision.
- Calculate each template correctly and expose missing inputs and zero denominators.
- Produce calendar-correct reports without turning partial periods into full actuals.
- Save and retrieve immutable snapshots; changed records never overwrite old ones.
- Refuse missing/expired/mismatched approval, unavailable capability and changed actions.
- Prevent duplicate/concurrent attempts; stop uncertain outcomes for reconciliation.
- Validate packaging against the official schema and run extracted runtime independently.
- Run automated tests on Windows/Linux, Python 3.11–3.13.
- Report host/production checks not actually performed.

## Product boundaries
No automated payment, contract signing, deletion, ad-account mutation or binding
communication connector. No automatic scheduling, hosted backend, mandatory paid API,
legal/tax certification, causal marketing forecast or self-modification. Advisory
fallback does not claim to have executed Python or verified source facts.

The private GitHub source and local distribution are authorized. Public publication,
broader sharing, live business actions and credential provisioning are separate choices.
