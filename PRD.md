# SoloOS — Solo Founder Operating System

**One Founder. One Company. One Intelligent Operating System.**

## Purpose
Help solo founders combine business intelligence, management reporting and explicitly
authorized execution. Prioritize a useful local skill before APIs or hosted services.
Original design focuses on evidence-aware management: data quality gates financial
claims, while each spending proposal separates arithmetic from management judgment.
Originality is a design goal, not a claim that no similar product exists.

## Users and scope
Target SaaS, e-commerce, creator, freelance, consulting, digital-products and custom
businesses. Phase 1 uses one fictional consulting business; business-type labels are
accepted but do not imply business-specific KPI templates exist.

Phase 1 delivers a company profile, validated cash transactions, conditional cash KPIs,
weekly executive report and CEO/CFO/CMO marketing review. Outputs are local artifacts;
there is no external execution, scheduling, backend, paid API or self-modification.

## Acceptance criteria
- Initialize fictional demo and empty real-company profile without fabricated actuals.
- Reject malformed money, dates, duplicate IDs, mismatched company/currency/timezone.
- Reproduce sample MYR 2,000 revenue, 2,700 expenses, -700 profit, 4.00 months runway.
- Explicitly distinguish missing evidence from confirmed zero activity.
- Produce a traceable seven-day executive report and a proposal for a monthly increase.
- Analyze MYR 600 increase at assumed 60% contribution margin: MYR 1,000 incremental
  break-even revenue and 3.33 months downside runway, not predicted performance.
- Refuse every external execution attempt, including requests carrying approval data.
- Run the skill copied outside the development repository and pass automated tests.

## Later product capabilities
Eight departments (CEO, CFO, CMO, Sales, COO, CTO/Product, HR/Partnership, BI), six
templates, custom KPIs, additional report cadences, decision outcomes, optional SQLite,
real approved connectors and plugin distribution require their planned phases.
Do not expand into Phase 2 without the founder's explicit approval.
